# Act I production briefs (merged)

Main route: the reconciled Environment Art Bible for Act I (`docs/AOTL_Act1_Environment_Art_Bible_Reconciled.png`).
Side areas, and the brief text of every bible zone: Production Pack v2 (`docs/production_pack_v2/Act_I_Production_Briefs.json`).
Owner decision 11-28: merge both. Palette (art bible): limestone ivory, royal blue, forest green, olive, earth brown, dark umber, violet.
Lighting: Present Age (worn and muted), Golden Age (regal and luminous), Cataclysm (chaotic and ashen), Unwritten (ethereal and distorted).

## 1. Crownsfall  (`crownsfall`, ACT01_Z01)
- **Quest:** Q_A01_01 A Bell Without a Ringer — meet archivist Mira Thorne; investigate missing royal courier Joren Pike and a bell heard by only a few citizens.
- **Look:** Warm limestone plaza, blue-gold banners, market terraces  ·  **Layer:** PRESENT  ·  **Kit:** City / Crownsfall (painted plaza)
- **Landmarks:** see map
- **Encounter:** talk — mira
- **Layout** (pack): Gate → market terrace → bell courtyard → archive; return loop via lower stair.
- **Encounter design** (pack): Teach movement on an open practice lane before the market investigation; optional conversations have no combat pressure.
- **Storytelling** (pack): A bell rope moves without a ringer; courier board has a scraped name.
- **Asset kit** (pack): Crown civic kit: limestone, slate, heraldry, awnings, fountains; retain approved camera and HUD.
- **Acceptance** (pack): Player can find Mira and trigger courier investigation without minimap; safe market is never a combat spawn.
