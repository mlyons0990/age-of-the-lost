# AOTL skill-icon standard — approved November 12, 2026

The user approved this as the style going forward for action-bar skill icons. Use the framed Higgsfield production batch `88f63d60-3742-48ad-846b-640315582612` as the authoritative visual reference.

Use the initial20-skill batch as the visual family, with Cinder Thread and the rear-view Ember Aura mage as the strongest style anchors. Production images use Higgsfield GPT Image 2.6 under the existing unlimited allowance. The earlier exploratory sources retain their original provider attribution.

Every icon is a finished square action illustration. Its oversized subject is deliberately cropped by the frame, and supporting artwork continues through the corners. There is no detached emblem, transparent margin, plain-color plate, text or fake UI. Each icon includes its own beveled dark-metal border, a near-black outer edge and narrow school-tinted inner trim, following the user’s WoW references. Godot supplies live keybinding, cooldown and resource-state overlays.

The artwork should feel deliberately illustrated: authored forms, confident painted light/shadow, subtle material color and crisp contours. Maintain AOTL's mature navy/brass/iron identity. Avoid cartoon gloss, perfect generic vector/3D forms, coarse brush streaks, surface-noise/crackle filters, tiny decorative particles, repetitive wispy effects and environmental scenery. Paint depth through meaningful forms, not a global texture effect.

| Visual family | Form and material | Readability cue |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Heavy steel, leather, brass, navy cloth | Broad weapon/shield silhouette; short ivory movement marks; warm dark recesses |
| Frost | Sharp pale cyan ice, cold transparent depths | Distinct needle/crown/pulse silhouettes; irregular illustrated shards |
| Fire | Directional hot core, orange/red flame forms | Clearly different thread/fan/sweep/ward/aura composition; no shared generic swirl |
| Arcane | Violet structures, portals, echoes and time forms | Strong bridge/crescent/hourglass geometry; selective pale-violet edges |
| Gold light | Ivory/gold star, sun and vertical impact | Star/solar/pillar silhouette remains distinct from both fire and physical metal |

These are art families, not new gameplay-school definitions. The real spell registry and the requested ability mechanic determine the subject. Do not add a school, ability, stat, rarity or unlock rule by naming an image.

For a future skill, supply its real or proposed ID, name, action and one dominant visual motif. Choose the closest existing family/reference, then change its silhouette, pose or direction so it remains distinguishable in the bar. Use `make_spell_brief.py` to create a single-image Higgsfield request. Future IDs stay concept-only until actual gameplay work implements them.

Review the resulting image at 32/64/128px, in grayscale and next to neighboring skills. Check a distinctive silhouette and mechanic, subject scale, clean corners/edges, coherent material light, school recognition and readability under cooldown/key overlays. Automated hash/opacity checks establish file integrity; they do not substitute for this visual review. Keep source, exact prompt, model/job ID, generation settings, reference provenance, selected crop and output hashes.

Generation settings: `gpt_image_2_6`, quality`medium`, resolution`2k`, background`opaque`, PNG, count 1, `use_unlim:true`. Quote first. If the allowance is unavailable or a request asks for credits, stop that request; never switch to a paid balance. Do not start a new subscription or trial.

The frame is part of the source illustration, around3–4% of each square per edge. Fire uses muted copper, frost cold steel, arcane amethyst steel, gold-light aged gold and physical iron/brass. Keep a coherent AOTL border family with clear bevel/corner separation, no neon halos or ornate clutter. Artwork fills the inside edge without padding.
