All selected textures use 128 pixels per square inventory cell. Sword is 128×384 (1×3 vertical), ring/amulet 128×128 (1×1), chest 256×384 (2×3), belt 256×128 (2×1). Other proposed footprints are recorded per entry. Proportional fitting preserves silhouettes; the image canvas communicates occupied cells without stretching the item to fill every corner.

The native Godot review project displays the selected PNGs over square cells. Left/right browse, click inspects, Esc returns. Six grid captures plus sword/ring inspection captures document the rendered review. The click and Escape inspection paths were exercised in the capture run. Image QA verifies exact canvas dimensions, RGBA format, nonempty artwork, export margins, transparent corners and no solid content touching selected source-region boundaries: 366/366 checks for 61 images. These are artwork checks, separate from the prior game's 749 checks.

The technical exporter never repaints or creates alpha masks. It selects source regions, measures the visible bounds (ignoring alpha below 8/255 only for measurement), crops, scales uniformly and centers. Generated sheets sometimes drifted across nominal grid gutters; explicit source rectangles preserve complete items while excluding neighbors. A tall tasselled cloth sash was replaced with a compact horizontal belt.
